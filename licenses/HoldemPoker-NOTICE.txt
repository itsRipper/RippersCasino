HoldemPoker.Evaluator
----------

A port of [HenryRLee/PokerHandEvaluator](https://github.com/HenryRLee/PokerHandEvaluator) from C++ to C#.
The code offers very efficient evaluation of Texas Hold'em Poker hand with 5 to 7 cards. It is developed by Mateusz Bąkała (bakala12@o2.pl)
