# RippersCasino

Six casino games for your SPT hideout, with NPC opponents, custom interfaces, animations and sound.

## Features

- Slots with jackpots and automatic spinning.
- Boss Racing featuring familiar Tarkov bosses.
- Blackjack against a dealer, with NPC players at the table.
- Texas Hold'em Poker against NPC opponents.
- European Roulette with a single-zero wheel.
- One-on-one Russian Roulette against an NPC.
- Persistent digital wallet and zero-fee RUB, USD and EUR exchange. One chip equals 1,000 RUB.

## Requirements

- SPT **4.1.6**.
- Intelligence Center **Level 1** and Generator **Level 1**.

## Installation

1. Download `RippersCasino-1.0.0.zip` from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/itsRipper/RippersCasino/releases).
2. Close Tarkov and the SPT server.
3. Extract the archive into your main SPT installation folder, beside `EscapeFromTarkov.exe`, merging the included directories.
4. Start SPT and enter your hideout.

See [installation and troubleshooting](INSTALL.md) for data locations and recovery guidance.

## Accessing the casino

Use the Intelligence Center laptop or select **Rippers Casino** from the Intelligence Center's interactions. Games run offline against NPCs where applicable.
